Built-in US English audio for the P5–P6 revision app.
Same voice and generation settings as the P3–P4 app: Kokoro-82M af_heart, en-us, speed 0.9; MP3 64 kbps.
Existing matching recordings are reused byte-for-byte. Additional recordings are synthesized locally with the same model and voices.bin.
Kokoro-82M: Apache-2.0, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
kokoro-onnx: MIT, https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx
Model binaries are not bundled.
